### 2026-10-21 (latest) — Subscription Retention card on the Subscriptions Stats page

- **Context:** Luis: "add retention rate." Data check first, because status alone gives a wrong answer: on prod, **101 of the 144 canceled rows never paid a single invoice** (trials that ended without payment), and **8 of the 10 past_due rows never paid either** (first payment failed after the trial). Counting those as churned subscribers would make retention look far worse than reality.
- **Definition used:** of everyone who has **ever paid at least one invoice** (`invoice_payments`), the share **still subscribed today** = rows with status `active` or `past_due` (past due = payment being retried, not canceled). Canceled-after-paying counts as lost. Never-paid rows (incomplete/expired checkouts, lapsed trials) and running trials are in neither bucket. Rate = retained ÷ (retained + churned). Prod snapshot: advanced 9 of 28 = 32.1%, basic 22 of 47 = 46.8%, overall 31 of 75 = 41.3%.
- **What changed:** new migration `20261021180000_admin_subscription_retention.sql` — CREATE OR REPLACE of `admin_subscription_stats` adding a `retention` array `[{ plan, retained, churned }]` computed with `EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM invoice_payments ...)` inside the existing `p_exclude`/`is_admin()` structure (everything else identical to `20261021170000`). Applied to staging + prod (HTTP 200; `prosrc` contains `v_retention` on both; the retention SELECT was run as plain SQL on prod and returned the numbers above before shipping). `SubscriptionsStatsPage` gained a "Subscription Retention" card after Free Trial Conversion: Overall + per-plan tiles (rate in green, caption "X of Y still subscribed") and a plain-English definition note; page subtitle now mentions retention.
- **Deliberate choices:** per-plan breakdown so Luis can compare plans (basic retains far better); the card is not clickable yet — the drill modals open per status × plan cell, and retention spans statuses plus payment history, so a drill here needs its own query; excluded the single `trialing`-but-paid edge row (a paying user in a new trial) to keep the definition crisp — it still appears in the trial section.
- **Drill-down added (same session, "do it"):** the Overall and per-plan tiles are now buttons that open a "Subscription Retention" modal — no new migration needed, because `invoice_payments` is already readable by admins (SELECT policy from `20261021130000`). The effect fetches three small queries on open: subscriptions with status active/past_due/canceled (optionally one plan, test-account filter applied at the query), `invoice_payments.user_id` for the ever-paid set, and `user_profiles` for names/join date/copier state — then filters client-side so the rows exactly match the rate's definition, sorts still-subscribed first (active, then past due, then lost) with each group newest first, and paginates client-side at the existing 50-row page size. Columns: User, Plan, Status, **Outcome** (green "Still subscribed" / orange "Lost after paying"), Copier, Joined (account sign-up from `user_profiles.created_at`, same as the trial modal), Trial Ended, Sub Started, Sub Ended (cancellation date for lost rows, current period end otherwise). "What you're seeing" box explains the grouping; test-account toggle honoured; profile rows capped at 500 ids like the other drills. ### 2026-10-21 (later) — Trial conversion section on the Subscriptions Stats page

- **Context:** Luis: "can we also see people that subscribed after their free trial ended vs those that did not." Data check first: trial info lives on `subscriptions.trial_ends_at` (there is no `users` table); payment truth lives on the new `invoice_payments` table (complete for all history after today's billing-backfill run on both projects).
- **Definitions (plain English):** a person counts as **subscribed after trial** when their newest subscription row has a trial and they have paid at least one invoice (Stripe converts trials at trial end, so any payment means the trial converted). **Trial ended, never paid** = trial end date has passed and there is not one payment on record. **Still in trial** = trial end in the future and no payment yet — shown for context, excluded from the rate. Conversion rate = converted / (converted + not converted).
- **What changed:** new migration `20261021150000_admin_trial_conversion_stats.sql` — `admin_trial_conversion_stats(p_exclude uuid[])` (SECURITY DEFINER + `is_admin()` guard, EXECUTE revoked from PUBLIC/anon, one row per person, `p_exclude` for test accounts, same pattern as the other stats RPCs). `SubscriptionsStatsPage` gained a "Free Trial Conversion" card with five tiles — **Subscribed after trial** (paid, still running), **Subscribed, then canceled** (paid, later canceled — split out at Luis's request so paying-then-churned people don't mix into the still-active list; they still count as converted in the rate), **Trial ended, never paid**, **Still in trial**, and **Conversion rate**; the first four open a drill-down modal (UserLink, plan, status, four dates — Joined = account sign-up from `user_profiles.created_at`, Trial Ended = `trial_ends_at`, Sub Started = subscription created, Sub Ended = `canceled_at` or current period end — shown as a dash while the person is still in a free trial (Stripe sets period end = trial end for trialing rows, which made the two columns identical; a not-yet-subscribed person has no subscription end date) — client-side pagination, "What you're seeing" explanation, test-account toggle honoured). Group splits are client-side over the one RPC payload (status-based); no extra DB round trips.
